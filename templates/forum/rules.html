Community rules

CsHub is for students of our department. Please help keep it useful and respectful.

  1. Be respectful. No insults, harassment, or hate speech.
  2. Feedback about teachers must be factual and polite. Describe your experience, not the person.
  3. Do not share personal data (phone numbers, private messages, or photos of other people).
  4. Post in the right community and search before asking a question that may already have an answer.
  5. No spam, advertising, or content unrelated to the department.
  6. If you see a post that breaks these rules, use the Report button. The admin may remove posts and deactivate accounts that break the rules.
